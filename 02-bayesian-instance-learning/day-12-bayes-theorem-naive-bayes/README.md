# Day 12 — Probability, Bayes' Rule, and Naive Bayes

**Module:** Module 3: Bayesian and Instance-Based Learning

## Theory

- Probability theory and conditional probability.
- Joint, marginal, and conditional probabilities.
- Bayes' theorem and Bayesian decision theory.
- Prior probability, likelihood, evidence, and posterior probability.
- Naive Bayes and its conditional-independence assumption.
- Laplace smoothing and the zero-frequency problem.

## Practical

- Solve a small Bayes' theorem problem by hand.
- Implement a simple spam classifier using MultinomialNB.
- Compare prior and posterior probabilities.
- Discuss why Naive Bayes can work well for text classification despite its simplifying assumptions.

## Outcome

Students can calculate conditional probabilities and build a basic Bayesian classifier.
