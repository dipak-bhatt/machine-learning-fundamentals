# Day 01 — Introduction to Machine Learning

**Module:** Module 1: Foundations

## Theory

- History and evolution of ML.
- Artificial intelligence vs. machine learning vs. deep learning.
- Definition, need, benefits, and limitations of ML.
- Biological neurons and artificial neurons: an intuitive introduction.
- Types of learning: supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, and reinforcement learning.
- Traditional programming vs. machine learning.

## Practical

- Set up Python, Jupyter Notebook or Google Colab.
- Introduce NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib.
- Load a small dataset and explore its rows, columns, and data types.
- Demonstrate a simple rule-based program versus a learned model.

## Outcome

Students can explain what ML is, identify its types, and recognize when it is useful.
