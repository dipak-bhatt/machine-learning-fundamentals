# Day 19 — ROC Curves, Statistical Uncertainty, and Model Comparison

**Module:** Module 5: Evaluation and Optimization

## Theory

- ROC curves and area under the curve.
- Precision-recall curves and their usefulness for rare positive cases.
- Confidence intervals and sampling uncertainty.
- Box plots and distributions of model performance.
- Cross-validation and stratified cross-validation.
- Comparing models fairly and avoiding test-set overuse.

## Practical

- Plot ROC and precision-recall curves.
- Compare two classifiers using cross-validation.
- Visualize performance across folds using box plots.
- Discuss whether a small difference in model scores is meaningful.

## Outcome

Learners can evaluate model stability and communicate uncertainty in performance.
