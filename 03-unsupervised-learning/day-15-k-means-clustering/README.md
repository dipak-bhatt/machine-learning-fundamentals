# Day 15 — Clustering and K-Means

**Module:** Module 4: Unsupervised Learning

## Theory

- Supervised vs. unsupervised learning.
- Clustering and its applications.
- K-means algorithm, centroids, and iterative assignment.
- Objective function and within-cluster sum of squares.
- Choosing k: elbow method and silhouette score.
- Initialization, sensitivity to outliers, and limitations of K-means.

## Practical

- Cluster customer records using selected numerical features.
- Visualize clusters with scatterplots.
- Apply the elbow method and silhouette score.
- Compare results under different initializations.

## Outcome

Students can run K-means, interpret its clusters, and understand why selecting k is not always straightforward.
